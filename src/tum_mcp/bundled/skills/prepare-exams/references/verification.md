# Verify Exam Prep

1. **Report**: after building, read `tum-mcp study exam-report PROJECT`. Resolve stale
   items, review low-confidence and out-of-scope questions, and decide every flagged
   target: revise it or keep it with a reason in your report.
2. **Content**: re-solve every changed answer, drill and forecast question; compare each
   cheatsheet line with its source; confirm every pattern workflow solves each listed
   instance.
3. **App**: open the changed views with `tum-mcp study` at a phone width (about 390px)
   and a desktop width. Check that cheatsheet lines read clearly without opening them,
   the map labels are legible, a guided past question works through its steps, and a
   practice exam finishes with sensible scoring. App behaviour itself is covered by
   the package's tests; report a suspected app defect instead of editing the app.
4. **Report** honestly: checks run, gaps (missing years, unreadable scans, unofficial
   solutions), kept outliers. A build does not prove correctness, and no check proves
   what the next exam contains.
