# Hands-free revision

The listener may be walking, unable to see formulas, or returning at a chapter.
Teach the prepared relationship and when it applies; do not read the page
paragraph by paragraph or demand speaking, tapping, writing or a paused player.
Automatic continuation must make sense even when the listener does not respond.

## Shape a passage

1. Name the subject and the decision or relationship it explains.
2. Establish only the objects and terms needed now. Replace “this”, “above” and
   “the second one” with named referents.
3. Explain the mechanism in connected steps; restate the state when the next step
   depends on remembering it.
4. Contrast confusable concepts using the same criterion. State the condition that
   selects each, not two detached definitions.
5. Return to the consequence and the assumptions that keep it valid.

Adapt this shape, not a fixed number of sentences. For a prepared probability
example, establish the sample space before restricting it; name the surviving
cases again before division. For a trace, name the current state and the rule
before the next update. Exact code syntax or spatial construction may require
Study: name that precise dependency rather than pretending speech replaces it.

## Re-entry and reinforcement

Each chapter opens with a subject and enough local context to stand alone.
“Now divide it by that” cannot begin a chapter even if its facts were explained
earlier. Test first/middle/last noninitial chapters directly, without the page.
Keep necessary signposts when adjacent chapters are played continuously.

Useful repetition restores state, emphasizes a condition, contrasts alternatives,
or revisits a relationship in a complementary way. It is not penalized by word
overlap or duration. Remove stock introductions and repeated full explanations
that add no orientation. A short reminder may prevent minutes of confusion.

## Conditions and delivery

Make negations, exceptions, units and quantifiers audible, especially where a
wrong choice would reverse the result. Introduce technical names naturally and
test their actual synthesized pronunciation. Pause around a difficult distinction;
do not speed up to fit a word/time target or stretch every sentence equally.
Optional source-backed recall has an automatic answer and needs no response.

Audition the complete final track without the page. Check beginning/end,
technical tokens, joins, fatigue, and automatic continuation. Re-enter chapters
and verify all referents. ASR can flag omitted words but cannot certify delivery.

## Coverage and review

Map source concepts to spoken sections/chapters with exact locators. Distinguish:
`spoken`, `study_required` (exact section and reason), and `missing`. A precise
modality limit may be kept and disclosed; omitted teachable meaning must be fixed.
Audio adaptation quality and audio-only completeness are different claims.

Use the eight-dimension [audio profile](../../review-course/references/media.md).
Never award Practice, Representation or Synchronization to ordinary audio.
Actual listening intervals and artifact/evidence hashes are required for delivery;
an unavailable listener leaves it unscored, not automatically poor or perfect.
